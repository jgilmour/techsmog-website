PermitReady is a free iOS app for teens preparing for their driver's permit (learner's permit) exam and for the supervised driving hours that follow it. It is the only iOS app that takes you from studying for your permit test all the way through logging your supervised driving hours — with no account, no sign-in, and no internet required. It covers all 50 U.S. states plus Washington, DC with state-specific practice questions drawn from official 2026 handbooks. Features include an untimed practice quiz, state-specific timed test simulations, study by category, offline access, progress tracking, and a driving hours log with parent sharing. The app is free with ads; a one-time $4.99 purchase removes ads with no subscriptions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which states are supported?

PermitReady supports all 50 U.S. states plus Washington, DC, with dedicated state-specific practice pages and handbook-based study content for every jurisdiction.

Does it work offline?

Yes! All question banks are bundled with the app, so you can study anywhere without an internet connection.

Are the questions similar to the real test?

Yes, our questions are based on official state driver handbooks (DMV, RMV, DPS, BMV, and other licensing agency materials) and cover all topics tested on your state's 2026 permit exam.

What's the difference between Practice Quiz and Practice Test?

Practice Quiz has no time limit and shows explanations after each answer, making it perfect for learning. Practice Test uses your state's question count and timed format when available, without explanations, to better match the real testing experience.

Is there a subscription?

No! PermitReady is free to use with ads. You can remove ads with a one-time $4.99 purchase—no subscriptions, no recurring fees.

Can I track my supervised driving hours?

Yes. PermitReady includes a driving hours log where you can record each supervised drive—the date, how long you drove, the conditions, and any notes. The app keeps a running total so you always know how close you are to your state's requirement.

Can my parent see my driving log?

Yes, if you send it to them. You can share your log as a file over AirDrop, Messages, Mail, or Files, and they can keep their own copy in the app. There's no account and no sign-in for either of you, and nothing is shared automatically.

How many supervised driving hours does my state require?

It varies—most states require somewhere between 40 and 60 hours, often with a minimum number at night. Check your state's page for its specific requirement, and always confirm with your state licensing agency.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No to both. PermitReady has no accounts, no sign-ups, and no logins. Every question bank and every drive you log is stored on your device, so the whole app works offline.

Does PermitReady send notifications?

Only if you ask for them. PermitReady never requests notification permission when you first open the app—you're asked only after you've finished a quiz, and only if you tap "Remind Me." You can save your permit test date for a check-in on the day, plus optional study reminders if you've been away.
