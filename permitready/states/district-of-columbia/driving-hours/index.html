District of Columbia Supervised Driving Hours Requirements

40 hours of supervised driving with 10 at night, certified by a parent — no separate test on the hours, just a signature and a record that backs it up.

TechSmog Apps › PermitReady › District of Columbia › Supervised Driving Hours

Washington, DC requires 40 hours of supervised driving, including at least 10 hours at night, before a teen can move from a learner permit to a provisional license. There is no test on the hours themselves — a parent or guardian certifies them, and the DMV takes that certification at face value. The knowledge test happens earlier, when the learner permit is issued. The learner permit has to be held for at least six months, and DC requires driver education for applicants under 18. DC is a single jurisdiction with one DMV, so there is no county-level variation to chase.

Who can supervise practice driving in District of Columbia

The supervision rule is standard. What makes DC different is the driving environment, which is about as demanding as anywhere in the country for a new driver.

  • A licensed driver at least 21 years old in the front passenger seat.
  • A licensed driving instructor qualifies during course sessions.
  • Learner permit restrictions apply, including limits on when a permit holder can drive. Check the current rules with the DMV so night practice happens inside them.

Night hours, and what "night" means here

Ten of the 40 hours have to be at night. The DC DMV does not publish a rigid clock definition, so treat night as actual darkness — after sunset, before sunrise. In DC, sunset is around 4:50 PM in December and after 8:35 PM in June, so the winter half of the permit period is the efficient window. Night driving in the District is a specific skill set: one-way grids that change direction, traffic circles that arrive with no warning, cyclists and scooters without lights, pedestrians crossing mid-block, and the reversible rush-hour lanes on Rock Creek Parkway and Connecticut Avenue. Add a bridge into Virginia and a run up North Capitol Street and you have covered more difficulty in an hour than most states manage in ten.

What counts as a loggable hour

  • Public-road driving with a qualified supervisor counts. The trip to school, errands, highway miles, practice loops around the neighborhood — it all counts as long as the permit holder is at the wheel and the supervising driver is in the front passenger seat.
  • Time parked, idling, or waiting in a drive-through does not count. Log driving time, not trip time.
  • Empty parking lots and closed courses generally do not count. The point of the requirement is real traffic.
  • Hours before the permit was issued do not count. The permit has to be in hand first.
  • Practice in all three jurisdictions if you can. A DC driver is in Maryland or Virginia within twenty minutes. Different speed limits, different turn-on-red rules, and very different road design — all of it worth meeting with a parent in the car.

How to actually get to 40 hours

Forty hours over the six-month permit period is about ninety minutes a week. In DC the constraint is usually the car, not the calendar — plenty of District families do not drive daily, so the hours have to be created rather than absorbed into an existing commute. A standing weekend session plus one evening drive gets there. Use the density: fifteen minutes of Dupont Circle traffic teaches more than an hour on a highway.

Frequently asked questions

How many supervised driving hours does Washington DC require?

40 hours, including at least 10 at night, before a provisional license. A parent or guardian certifies the total on the application.

Is there a test on the driving hours in DC?

No. The hours are certified by a parent or guardian rather than tested. The knowledge test happens earlier, when the learner permit is issued, and the road test comes at the end.

How long do you have to hold a DC learner permit?

Six months before applying for a provisional license.

How many night hours does DC require?

10 of the 40. Treat night as actual darkness between sunset and sunrise. Winter evenings, when it is dark before 5 PM, are the efficient window.

Is driver education required in DC?

Yes, for applicants under 18. Its behind-the-wheel instruction is separate from the 40 supervised hours.
