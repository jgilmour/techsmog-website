Idaho Supervised Driving Hours Requirements

50 hours of supervised driving with 10 at night, on a Supervised Instruction Permit that starts as early as 14½.

TechSmog Apps › PermitReady › Idaho › Supervised Driving Hours

Idaho requires 50 hours of supervised driving, including at least 10 hours at night, for drivers under 17. Idaho is one of the states where driver education comes first: teens between 14½ and 17 must complete an approved driver education course before they can get a Supervised Instruction Permit, and the permit has to be held for at least six months. A parent or guardian signs a driving log certifying the 50 hours, and that log is turned in when you apply for the license.

Who can supervise practice driving in Idaho

Idaho's rule is the standard one, with one detail that matters because permits start so young.

  • A licensed driver at least 21 years old in the front passenger seat.
  • The driver education instructor supervises during course behind-the-wheel sessions; those instruction hours are counted separately from the 50.
  • Idaho permits can be issued at 14½, which means a lot of practice happens with a very inexperienced driver. The 21-and-over rule is not a formality on rural highways.

Night hours, and what "night" means here

Ten of the 50 hours have to be at night, and Idaho actually turns in a log, so this number gets read. ITD does not publish a rigid clock definition, so use actual darkness — after sunset, before sunrise. Idaho's seasonal swing is significant: in Boise, December darkness starts a little after 5 PM while June light lasts past 9:30 PM. Bank the night hours between October and March if you can. Idaho-specific conditions worth getting in the dark: two-lane highways with no shoulder lighting, deer and elk crossings at dusk, and winter roads where the plowed edge and the lane line are the same color.

What counts as a loggable hour

  • Public-road driving with a qualified supervisor counts. The trip to school, errands, highway miles, practice loops around the neighborhood — it all counts as long as the permit holder is at the wheel and the supervising driver is in the front passenger seat.
  • Time parked, idling, or waiting in a drive-through does not count. Log driving time, not trip time.
  • Empty parking lots and closed courses generally do not count. The point of the requirement is real traffic.
  • Hours before the permit was issued do not count. The permit has to be in hand first.
  • Winter driving is the Idaho skill. The state does not require inclement-weather hours, but ice on a canyon road is what actually kills new Idaho drivers. Log those drives specifically so you know they happened.

How to actually get to 50 hours

Fifty hours over the six-month permit period is about two hours a week. The Idaho pattern that works is attaching driving to something already scheduled — the drive to practice, the run into town, the Sunday trip to a bigger grocery store. Families in rural counties often have the opposite problem from suburban ones: plenty of highway miles, very little practice at intersections and in traffic. If your fifty hours are all open road, spend a few sessions in Boise, Idaho Falls, or Coeur d'Alene before the road test.

Frequently asked questions

How many supervised driving hours does Idaho require?

50 hours, including at least 10 at night, for drivers under 17. A parent or guardian signs a driving log that is submitted with the license application.

Does Idaho require driver education first?

Yes, for drivers under 17. An approved driver education course has to be started before a Supervised Instruction Permit is issued, which is why Idaho permits can begin as early as 14 and a half.

How many night hours does Idaho require?

10 of the 50. Treat night as actual darkness between sunset and sunrise. Idaho's short winter days make October through March the efficient window.

How long is an Idaho Supervised Instruction Permit valid?

Commonly 180 days, with renewal available. The permit also has to be held for at least six months before the license, so the two periods line up closely — watch the expiration date.

Does Idaho have an official driving log form?

Yes. Idaho requires a signed supervised driving log documenting the 50 hours. Get the current version from ITD or your driver education provider.
