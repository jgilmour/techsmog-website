New York Supervised Driving Hours Requirements

50 hours of supervised driving with 15 at night — one of the highest night-hour requirements in the country.

TechSmog Apps › PermitReady › New York › Supervised Driving Hours

New York requires 50 hours of supervised driving, including at least 15 hours after sunset and before sunrise, before a driver under 18 can take the road test. The hours are certified by a parent or guardian on form MV-262. Fifteen night hours is more than most states ask for. Source: New York DMV.

Who may supervise driving practice in New York

  • A licensed driver age 21 or older, holding a valid license for the class of vehicle being driven.
  • Seated in the front passenger seat for the entire drive.
  • A licensed driving instructor during a lesson.

New York layers regional restrictions on top of the supervision rule — junior permit holders face different limits in New York City, on Long Island, and in Westchester County than upstate. Those rules govern when and where you can practice, and they do not change the 50-hour requirement, but they do change how easy the hours are to accumulate.

What counts as a loggable hour

  • Public-road driving with a qualified supervisor counts — highway, city, suburban, and the school run all included.
  • Night is defined by sunset and sunrise, not by a fixed clock time. A 6:30 PM drive in January is a night hour; the same drive in July is not. Northern New York gets very early winter sunsets, which is the easiest way to bank the 15 hours.
  • Parking lots and private property generally do not count.
  • Time parked or idling does not count. Log the driving, not the outing.
  • Hours before the permit was issued do not count.
  • Driver education behind-the-wheel time is supervised driving with a licensed instructor and is generally loggable — confirm with your school and the DMV how it should appear on MV-262.

Study tip for the New York permit test

Worth knowing before you get to the hours: New York's written test includes a road sign section that is scored separately, and you generally need all 4 road sign questions correct. You can miss a handful of the general questions and still pass; miss too many signs and you fail regardless of the rest. Study road signs as their own category rather than trusting them to come along with everything else — PermitReady's New York practice has a signs-only mode for exactly this.

Frequently asked questions

How many supervised driving hours does New York require?

50 hours, including at least 15 after sunset and before sunrise, certified by a parent or guardian on form MV-262.

Why does New York require 15 night hours?

Most states set the night minimum at 10. New York sets it at 15, which is among the highest in the country. Practically it means about three of every ten hours you log need to be after dark, so start banking them in winter rather than saving them for the end.

What is form MV-262?

The New York State Certification of Supervised Driving — the form a parent or guardian signs certifying the 50 hours and 15 night hours. You bring it to the road test.

Does New York accept a digital driving log?

The signed MV-262 is what the DMV asks for, so a digital log supplements it rather than replaces it. Track in the app, transfer the totals to the form, get it signed. Confirm current requirements with the NY DMV.

Who can supervise my practice driving?

A licensed driver 21 or older in the front passenger seat. Regional junior-permit restrictions in NYC, Long Island, and Westchester also affect where and when you can practice.

Can I log hours before I get my permit?

No. Hours count from the day the learner permit is issued, and drivers under 18 must hold the permit at least 6 months before the road test.
