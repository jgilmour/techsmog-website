Nebraska Supervised Driving Hours Requirements

50 hours of supervised driving with 10 of them between sunset and sunrise — certified on Nebraska's 50-hour form.

TechSmog Apps › PermitReady › Nebraska › Supervised Driving Hours

Nebraska requires 50 hours of supervised driving, including at least 10 hours between sunset and sunrise, before a teen can get a Provisional Operator's Permit at 16. Nebraska is one of the few states that defines night explicitly as sunset to sunrise rather than leaving it open, which removes the guesswork. A parent or guardian signs the 50-hour certification. Completing an approved driver safety course is an alternative to the 50-hour certification. Nebraska also offers a School Permit at 14 for eligible rural students.

Who can supervise practice driving in Nebraska

Nebraska's supervision rule is standard; the School Permit is the piece that makes Nebraska different.

  • A licensed driver at least 21 years old in the front passenger seat.
  • A licensed driving instructor qualifies during a lesson.
  • The School Permit is a separate track. Eligible students in rural areas can drive to and from school unsupervised at 14 under narrow conditions. That is not a substitute for the supervised hours needed for a Provisional Operator's Permit.

Night hours, and what "night" means here

Nebraska says sunset to sunrise, in those words, which is more helpful than the vague "night" most states use — you can look up the exact time for any date and know precisely whether a drive counted. Ten hours is roughly twenty half-hour drives. In Omaha and Lincoln, sunset lands around 5 PM in December and after 9 PM in late June, so the winter half of the permit period is where these hours are cheap. Nebraska night conditions worth seeking out: unlit gravel and county roads, highway stretches where the only lights for miles are grain elevators, and the deer that come out of the ditch at dusk in the fall.

What counts as a loggable hour

  • Public-road driving with a qualified supervisor counts. The trip to school, errands, highway miles, practice loops around the neighborhood — it all counts as long as the permit holder is at the wheel and the supervising driver is in the front passenger seat.
  • Time parked, idling, or waiting in a drive-through does not count. Log driving time, not trip time.
  • Empty parking lots and closed courses generally do not count. The point of the requirement is real traffic.
  • Hours before the permit was issued do not count. The permit has to be in hand first.
  • Sunset and sunrise are lookup-able. Because Nebraska defines night precisely, there is no ambiguity to argue about — check the time for your date and log accordingly.

How to actually get to 50 hours

Fifty hours is about two hours a week over a six-month stretch. In rural Nebraska those hours accumulate quickly on highway trips, which is a mixed blessing: highway miles are easy but low-value practice. Balance them with town driving in Lincoln, Omaha, Grand Island, or Kearney, where the intersections and traffic actually build judgment.

Frequently asked questions

How many supervised driving hours does Nebraska require?

50 hours, including at least 10 hours between sunset and sunrise, before a Provisional Operator's Permit at 16. An approved driver safety course can be taken in place of the 50-hour certification.

How does Nebraska define night driving?

Sunset to sunrise, stated explicitly. That is more precise than most states, and it means you can look up the exact time for any date and know whether a drive counts.

What is the Nebraska School Permit?

A permit available at 14 for eligible students, mostly in rural areas, allowing limited unsupervised driving to and from school. It is a separate track and does not replace the supervised hours needed for a Provisional Operator's Permit.

Does Nebraska have an official driving log form?

Nebraska requires a 50-hour certification signed by a parent or guardian and submitted with the Provisional Operator's Permit application. Get the current form from the DMV.

Who can supervise practice driving in Nebraska?

A licensed driver at least 21 years old, seated in the front passenger seat. A licensed driving instructor also qualifies.
