Massachusetts Supervised Driving Hours Requirements

40 hours of supervised driving, 10 of them at night — or 30 hours with an RMV-certified driver skills development program.

TechSmog Apps › PermitReady › Massachusetts › Supervised Driving Hours

Massachusetts requires 40 hours of supervised driving, including at least 10 hours at night, before a driver under 18 can apply for a Junior Operator License. The total drops to 30 hours for teens who complete an RMV-certified driver skills development program. A parent or guardian certifies the hours. Source: Massachusetts RMV.

Who may supervise driving practice in Massachusetts

The person in the passenger seat has to meet all three of these:

  • Age 21 or older. Not 18, not an older sibling with a fresh license.
  • At least one year of driving experience on a valid license.
  • Seated in the front passenger seat for the entire drive, close enough to take control of the wheel.

A driving instructor supervising a lesson also satisfies the supervision rule. What does not count: a licensed adult riding in the back seat, or a supervisor who has stepped out of the car while the permit holder moves it.

What counts as a loggable hour

Generally, any time the permit holder is actively driving on a public road with a qualified supervisor in the front passenger seat counts. That includes highway driving, the trip to school, errands, and practice loops around the neighborhood.

  • Highway and city driving both count. Massachusetts does not restrict practice to specific road types, and mixing conditions is exactly what the road test expects.
  • Night hours are counted separately. Massachusetts sets a 10-hour night minimum, so tag night drives as you go. Practically, drives after dark are what the RMV log's night column is for; the safest interpretation is actual darkness between sunset and sunrise, and evening drives between 7 PM and midnight in winter will comfortably qualify.
  • Parking lots and closed courses generally do not count. The requirement is behind-the-wheel experience on public roads.
  • Time parked, idling, or waiting in a drive-through does not count. Log driving time, not trip time.
  • Hours before the permit was issued do not count. The permit must be in hand first.

Tracking your hours

The Massachusetts RMV references RoadReady by name in its official materials and offers a free downloadable state log form at mass.gov. You can use the state's paper form, RoadReady, or PermitReady to track your hours — but confirm with the RMV which format they accept.

PermitReady is a free iPhone and iPad app that does two things: practice questions for the Massachusetts RMV permit test, and a supervised driving hours log. Each drive is one entry — date, duration, and condition tags including night, highway, rain, and heavy traffic — with a running total at the top so you always know how close you are to 40 (or 30). Entries can be backdated, so a log started late can still be complete.

Frequently asked questions

How many supervised driving hours does Massachusetts require?

40 hours, including at least 10 at night, before applying for a Junior Operator License. Teens who complete an RMV-certified driver skills development program need 30 hours instead.

How many night hours does Massachusetts require?

10 of the 40. Night hours are recorded in their own column on the RMV log, so tag them drive by drive rather than estimating at the end. Ten hours is roughly twenty half-hour evening drives — easier in November than in June.

Does Massachusetts have an official driving log form?

Yes. The RMV publishes a free supervised driving log PDF at mass.gov. Massachusetts is one of the minority of states that provides one, which makes it the safest format to show up with.

Does Massachusetts accept a digital driving log?

The RMV references the RoadReady app by name in its official materials, which tells you digital tracking is recognized in principle. The RMV also publishes a paper form. Either way the hours are certified by a parent or guardian, so confirm the accepted format with the RMV before your road test.

Who can supervise my practice driving?

A licensed driver 21 or older with at least one year of driving experience, seated in the front passenger seat.

Can I log hours before I get my permit?

No. Hours count only after the learner's permit is issued. Massachusetts also requires holding the permit for 6 months before the road test, so the clock and the hours run together.
