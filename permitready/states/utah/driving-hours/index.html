Utah Supervised Driving Hours Requirements

40 hours of supervised driving with 10 of them after sunset, alongside Utah's required driver education course.

TechSmog Apps › PermitReady › Utah › Supervised Driving Hours

Utah requires 40 hours of supervised driving, including at least 10 hours after sunset, before a teen can get a license at 16. Driver education is required for drivers under 19, and the learner permit has to be held for at least six months. A parent or guardian certifies the hours — usually on the log that comes with the driver education packet. Utah's phrasing of the night requirement is a bit more concrete than most states': it points at sunset rather than a vague notion of "night."

Who can supervise practice driving in Utah

Utah's rule is standard, and the driver education course is where most families get the log form in the first place.

  • A licensed driver at least 21 years old — a parent, guardian, or responsible adult — in the front passenger seat.
  • A certified driving instructor qualifies during course sessions.
  • Instructor hours and supervised hours are different. The behind-the-wheel time in driver education does not count toward the 40.

Night hours, and what "night" means here

Utah asks for 10 hours after sunset, which is a cleaner standard than most states' unqualified "night" — sunset is a specific, lookup-able time for any date. In Salt Lake City that means anything after about 5:05 PM in December and after 9 PM in late June, so the winter half of the year is dramatically more efficient. Utah-specific conditions worth doing in the dark: canyon roads where the grade and the curves come together, winter inversions where visibility drops and roads stay icy all day, and the long unlit stretches of I-15 and I-70 where the only reference is your own headlights.

What counts as a loggable hour

  • Public-road driving with a qualified supervisor counts. The trip to school, errands, highway miles, practice loops around the neighborhood — it all counts as long as the permit holder is at the wheel and the supervising driver is in the front passenger seat.
  • Time parked, idling, or waiting in a drive-through does not count. Log driving time, not trip time.
  • Empty parking lots and closed courses generally do not count. The point of the requirement is real traffic.
  • Hours before the permit was issued do not count. The permit has to be in hand first.
  • Canyon and mountain driving deserves its own attention. Utah does not require it, but downhill grades, runaway-truck ramps, and snowpack on a canyon road are ordinary here and nothing like valley driving.

How to actually get to 40 hours

Forty hours over the six-month permit period is about ninety minutes a week. The night share is the constraint, especially for a permit issued in spring: 10 hours after sunset in June means driving after 9 PM, which most families will not do. Front-load the after-sunset hours in whatever dark months your permit period covers.

Frequently asked questions

How many supervised driving hours does Utah require?

40 hours, including at least 10 after sunset, before a license at 16. A parent or guardian certifies the hours on a driving log.

How does Utah define night driving?

Utah's requirement points at hours after sunset, which is more precise than the vague "night" most states use. Sunset time is lookup-able for any date, so there is no ambiguity about whether a drive counted.

Is driver education required in Utah?

Yes, for drivers under 19. The course includes classroom and behind-the-wheel instruction, and those instructor hours are separate from the 40 supervised hours.

How long do you have to hold a Utah learner permit?

Six months before applying for a license at 16.

Does Utah have an official driving log form?

Yes. Utah requires a certified driving log documenting the 40 hours. Driver education providers and the Driver License Division supply the form.
