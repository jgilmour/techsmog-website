South Dakota Supervised Driving Hours Requirements

Supervised hours with a night minimum and inclement-weather practice — South Dakota is one of the few states that names bad weather in the requirement.

TechSmog Apps › PermitReady › South Dakota › Supervised Driving Hours

South Dakota requires supervised driving hours that include both night driving and driving in inclement weather before a teen can move from an instruction permit to a restricted minor's permit. The commonly cited figures are 50 hours total, with 10 at night and 10 in inclement conditions. South Dakota is one of only a handful of states that names bad weather in the requirement at all — a sensible rule in a state where a January drive is a different activity from a July one. The instruction permit period is shorter if driver education is completed: commonly 180 days with a course, longer without.

Who can supervise practice driving in South Dakota

South Dakota's permit age of 14 means practice starts very young, and the state's rural roads are unforgiving of inattention in the passenger seat.

  • A licensed adult in the front passenger seat for every drive — in practice, a parent, guardian, or an adult they authorize.
  • A driver education instructor qualifies during course sessions.
  • Starting at 14 gives you two years. That is an advantage worth using, especially because the inclement-weather hours require living through a winter.

Night hours, and what "night" means here

South Dakota asks for night hours and inclement-weather hours, which is a rare and honest pairing. Treat night as actual darkness — after sunset, before sunrise. In Sioux Falls and Rapid City, December darkness starts around 4:55 PM, so any after-school drive counts from November through February. The inclement-weather requirement is the harder one, and it is also the point: blowing snow on an open plain, ice on a bridge deck, and gravel roads that turn to slush are the conditions that put new South Dakota drivers in ditches. Those hours cannot be scheduled — you take them when the weather gives them to you.

What counts as a loggable hour

  • Public-road driving with a qualified supervisor counts. The trip to school, errands, highway miles, practice loops around the neighborhood — it all counts as long as the permit holder is at the wheel and the supervising driver is in the front passenger seat.
  • Time parked, idling, or waiting in a drive-through does not count. Log driving time, not trip time.
  • Empty parking lots and closed courses generally do not count. The point of the requirement is real traffic.
  • Hours before the permit was issued do not count. The permit has to be in hand first.
  • Weather hours are opportunistic. When it is snowing and you have thirty minutes, that is the drive to take. Waiting for a convenient blizzard in April does not work.

How to actually get to 50 hours

Fifty hours over a six-month permit period is about two hours a week, which is manageable. The requirement that actually sets the calendar is the weather: a permit issued in March means almost no chance of inclement practice before summer. If you have the flexibility, start the permit in the fall so the winter does the hard part for you.

Frequently asked questions

How many supervised driving hours does South Dakota require?

The commonly cited figure is 50 hours, including 10 at night and 10 in inclement conditions. Confirm the current requirement with the South Dakota Department of Public Safety before applying.

Does South Dakota require bad-weather driving hours?

Yes, South Dakota is one of the few states that names inclement conditions in its requirement. Those hours cannot be scheduled, so take them opportunistically when the weather turns.

How old do you have to be to get a South Dakota instruction permit?

14, which is among the youngest in the country. That gives most families two full years of practice, which is useful given the weather requirement.

How long do you have to hold a South Dakota instruction permit?

Commonly 180 days with an approved driver education course, and longer without one. Confirm the current periods with the DPS.

Does South Dakota have an official driving log form?

South Dakota requires certification of the supervised hours signed by a parent or guardian. Get the current form from the Department of Public Safety.
