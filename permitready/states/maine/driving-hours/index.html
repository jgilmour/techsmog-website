Maine Supervised Driving Hours Requirements

70 hours of supervised driving with 10 at night — second only to Oregon, and 35 of the 70 have to come after driver education ends.

TechSmog Apps › PermitReady › Maine › Supervised Driving Hours

Maine requires 70 hours of supervised driving, including at least 10 hours at night, before a teen can get an intermediate license. That total is the second highest in the country behind Oregon. Maine also requires driver education for anyone under 21 — commonly 30 hours of classroom, 10 hours behind the wheel, and 6 hours of observation — and the permit has to be held for at least six months. A parent or guardian certifies the supervised hours.

Who can supervise practice driving in Maine

Maine sets the supervisor age lower than most states but adds an experience requirement.

  • At least 20 years old and licensed for at least two years — Maine's age floor is 20, not 21, but the two-year experience rule still rules out recently licensed adults.
  • A driver education instructor qualifies during course sessions.
  • Front passenger seat, every drive.

Night hours, and what "night" means here

Ten of the 70 hours have to be at night. Maine's latitude makes this the easiest night requirement in the country to satisfy for half the year and the hardest for the other half: in December, Portland is dark by 4:05 PM, so the drive home from anything is a night drive. In June, sunset is after 8:20 PM. If your permit period spans a winter, the night hours will accumulate whether you plan them or not. What is worth doing deliberately: unlit Route 1 and inland two-lanes after dark, where moose and deer are a genuine hazard and the reflective markers are the only edge reference you get.

What counts as a loggable hour

  • Public-road driving with a qualified supervisor counts. The trip to school, errands, highway miles, practice loops around the neighborhood — it all counts as long as the permit holder is at the wheel and the supervising driver is in the front passenger seat.
  • Time parked, idling, or waiting in a drive-through does not count. Log driving time, not trip time.
  • Empty parking lots and closed courses generally do not count. The point of the requirement is real traffic.
  • Hours before the permit was issued do not count. The permit has to be in hand first.
  • Winter road hours are the Maine skill. Snow, ice, and frost heaves are not on the state's list, but they are the conditions a new Maine driver will meet in their first solo month.

How to actually get to 70 hours

Seventy hours over a six-month permit period is close to three hours a week — the heaviest weekly pace of any state except Oregon. That is not something you fit in around the edges; it needs a schedule. The families that manage it treat every routine trip as a driving lesson from the day the permit is issued: school, groceries, hockey practice, the dump run. If the permit period is longer than six months, spread it out and the pace drops fast.

Tracking your hours

Seventy hours is a lot of driving to remember. Maine's certification is signed by a parent, and the only honest way to sign a number that big is to have written it down along the way.

Frequently asked questions

How many supervised driving hours does Maine require?

70 hours, including at least 10 at night, before an intermediate license. Only Oregon's 100-hour requirement is higher.

Who can supervise practice driving in Maine?

A licensed driver at least 20 years old who has held a license for at least two years, or a driver education instructor, seated in the front passenger seat.

How many night hours does Maine require?

10 of the 70. Maine's early winter darkness — sunset around 4:05 PM in December — makes those hours easy to collect if the permit period spans a winter.

Is driver education required in Maine?

Yes, for drivers under 21. The course commonly runs 30 hours of classroom instruction, 10 hours behind the wheel, and 6 hours of observation, all separate from the 70 supervised hours.

Does Maine have an official driving log form?

Maine requires certification of the supervised driving experience signed by a parent or guardian. Get the current form from the BMV or your driver education provider.
