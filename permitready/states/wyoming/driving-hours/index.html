Wyoming Supervised Driving Hours Requirements

50 hours of supervised driving with 10 at night, certified by a parent, before the intermediate permit.

TechSmog Apps › PermitReady › Wyoming › Supervised Driving Hours

Wyoming requires 50 hours of supervised driving, including at least 10 hours at night, before a teen can move from a learner permit to an intermediate permit. A parent or guardian certifies the hours. Wyoming's learner permit is available at 15, and the state's holding period is shorter than most — confirm the current period with WYDOT Driver Services, since it is the detail most often misreported. Wyoming's long distances and severe winter weather make the practice more consequential here than the numbers suggest.

Who can supervise practice driving in Wyoming

Wyoming's supervision rule is looser than most states', which fits a state where many teens have been driving on ranch land for years. Confirm the current wording before you rely on it.

  • A licensed adult in the front passenger seat for every drive.
  • A driver education instructor qualifies during course sessions.
  • Ranch and farm driving is not the same as road driving. A teen who has been driving a pickup around a property since they were twelve still needs the highway, town, and night hours — those are the parts that kill people here.

Night hours, and what "night" means here

Ten of the 50 hours have to be at night. WYDOT does not publish a rigid clock definition, so treat night as actual darkness — after sunset, before sunrise. In Cheyenne and Casper, December is dark by about 4:35 PM, which makes the winter half of the year the efficient window. Wyoming's night driving is genuinely demanding: I-80 and I-25 at night with heavy truck traffic, crosswinds that shove a light vehicle across a lane, antelope and deer on unlit two-lanes, and stretches where there are no lights of any kind for forty miles. Ten hours is a minimum, and Wyoming is a state where exceeding it is obviously worth it.

What counts as a loggable hour

  • Public-road driving with a qualified supervisor counts. The trip to school, errands, highway miles, practice loops around the neighborhood — it all counts as long as the permit holder is at the wheel and the supervising driver is in the front passenger seat.
  • Time parked, idling, or waiting in a drive-through does not count. Log driving time, not trip time.
  • Empty parking lots and closed courses generally do not count. The point of the requirement is real traffic.
  • Hours before the permit was issued do not count. The permit has to be in hand first.
  • Wind and ice deserve deliberate practice. Wyoming does not require weather hours, but a ground blizzard on I-80 is an ordinary February event and the single most dangerous thing a new Wyoming driver will face.

How to actually get to 50 hours

Fifty hours goes fast in Wyoming, because a single trip between towns can be two hours. That is the trap: 50 hours of straight interstate is not the same education as 50 hours that include town intersections, parking, merging, and winter roads. Deliberately spend part of the total in Cheyenne, Casper, or Laramie traffic, and part of it in weather.

Frequently asked questions

How many supervised driving hours does Wyoming require?

50 hours, including at least 10 at night, before an intermediate permit. A parent or guardian certifies the total.

How long do you have to hold a Wyoming learner permit?

Wyoming's holding period is shorter than most states'. Confirm the current requirement with WYDOT Driver Services, since this detail is often misreported by third-party sites.

How many night hours does Wyoming require?

10 of the 50. Given unlit interstates, crosswinds, and wildlife, most Wyoming families treat 10 as a floor rather than a target.

Does ranch driving count toward Wyoming's hours?

The requirement is about supervised driving on public roads with a licensed adult in the front passenger seat. Private-property driving experience is useful but is not what the certification is describing.

Does Wyoming have an official driving log form?

Wyoming requires certification of the 50 hours signed by a parent or guardian. Get the current form from WYDOT Driver Services.
