California Supervised Driving Hours Requirements

50 hours of supervised practice with 10 at night, plus 6 hours with a professional instructor — and no mandatory state form.

TechSmog Apps › PermitReady › California › Supervised Driving Hours

California requires 50 hours of supervised driving, including at least 10 hours at night, before a driver under 18 can take the driving test. Night is defined as 11 PM to 5 AM. Separately, applicants must complete 6 hours of behind-the-wheel training with a professional instructor. Source: California DMV.

Who may supervise driving practice in California

  • A California-licensed driver age 25 or older — note that California's threshold is 25, not the 21 used by most states.
  • A licensed parent, guardian, spouse, or employer also qualifies to supervise.
  • Seated in the front passenger seat, close enough to take control of the vehicle at any time.
  • A licensed driving instructor during the required 6 hours of professional training.

The 25-year-old rule catches families out. An aunt who is 23 and has been driving for seven years is not a valid supervisor in California unless she is the permit holder's guardian or spouse.

What counts as a loggable hour

  • Driving on public roads with a qualified supervisor counts — freeway, surface streets, canyon roads, the lot.
  • Night means 11 PM to 5 AM in California. This is the detail people get wrong most often. A 7:30 PM drive in December is dark, but it is not a California night hour. Ten hours between 11 PM and 5 AM takes planning — late returns from events, early-morning airport runs, and deliberate late drives with a parent.
  • The 6 professional hours are separate. Do not count driving-school time toward the 50, and do not assume the 50 covers the 6.
  • Parking lots and private property generally do not count.
  • Parked and idling time does not count.
  • Nothing before the instruction permit was issued counts.

Tracking your hours

California is friendlier than most states for digital logs: there is no mandatory state form. The parent or guardian certifies the hours as part of the license application, and the DMV simply recommends that families keep a log. That means a spreadsheet, a notebook, or an app record are all acceptable ways to keep the record you certify from — there is no format to match.

Frequently asked questions

How many supervised driving hours does California require?

50 hours, including 10 at night, plus 6 separate hours of professional behind-the-wheel instruction, for drivers under 18.

How does California define a night hour?

Driving between 11 PM and 5 AM. Most states use sunset to sunrise, so California's window is significantly narrower and the 10 night hours take real planning.

Does California require an official log form?

No. There is no mandatory state log form. A parent or guardian certifies the hours on the license application, and the DMV recommends keeping a log in whatever format works for you — paper, spreadsheet, or app.

Do the 6 driving-school hours count toward the 50?

No. They are separate requirements. You need both.

Who can supervise my practice driving in California?

A licensed driver 25 or older, or a licensed parent, guardian, spouse, or employer, in the front passenger seat. California's age threshold is higher than the 21 most states use.

Can I log hours before I get my permit?

No. Hours count from the day the instruction permit is issued, and under-18 drivers must hold it at least 6 months before the driving test.
