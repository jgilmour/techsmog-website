Colorado Supervised Driving Hours Requirements

50 hours of supervised driving with 10 at night, plus a full 12 months holding the permit — the longest waiting period of any state.

TechSmog Apps › PermitReady › Colorado › Supervised Driving Hours

Colorado requires 50 hours of supervised driving, including at least 10 hours at night, before a teen can apply for a minor driver's license at 16. Colorado also requires the permit to be held for a full 12 months, which is twice the six-month period most states use and the longest in the country. A parent or guardian signs a log or affidavit certifying the hours. Because the permit period is a year, the hours are rarely the bottleneck in Colorado — the calendar is.

Who can supervise practice driving in Colorado

Colorado's supervision rule is standard, but the paperwork around it is heavier than most states', because the same parent who signs the log also signs the affidavit taking on financial liability.

  • A licensed driver at least 21 years old in the front passenger seat — in practice usually a parent, guardian, or licensed instructor.
  • The affidavit of liability matters. The adult who signs for a Colorado minor is accepting financial responsibility for the teen's driving until they turn 18 or the affidavit is withdrawn.
  • Alternate-permit holders and driving school instructors can supervise lessons; check the DMV's current rules if the supervising adult is not a parent.

Night hours, and what "night" means here

Ten of the 50 hours have to be at night. Colorado does not lock down a clock definition, so read it as actual darkness — after sunset and before sunrise. Colorado's geography makes this a more useful requirement than it sounds: mountain driving after dark, with unlit switchbacks, deer at the shoulder, and black ice from October through April, is a completely different skill from the same road at noon. If you live on the Front Range, get at least a few of the night hours on I-70 or a canyon road rather than doing all ten on lit suburban streets. Winter helps — darkness starts around 4:45 PM in December, so an after-school drive counts.

What counts as a loggable hour

  • Public-road driving with a qualified supervisor counts. The trip to school, errands, highway miles, practice loops around the neighborhood — it all counts as long as the permit holder is at the wheel and the supervising driver is in the front passenger seat.
  • Time parked, idling, or waiting in a drive-through does not count. Log driving time, not trip time.
  • Empty parking lots and closed courses generally do not count. The point of the requirement is real traffic.
  • Hours before the permit was issued do not count. The permit has to be in hand first.
  • Altitude and weather drives are worth marking. Colorado does not require bad-weather hours, but snow, ice, and mountain grades are the conditions that matter here. A log entry noting "snow, I-70 westbound" tells you something a bare hour count does not.

How to actually get to 50 hours

Fifty hours across a 12-month permit period is one hour a week. That is the gentlest pace of any state, and it is why Colorado families rarely miss the hours — they miss the log. The mistake is going eleven months without writing anything down and then trying to reconstruct a year of driving the night before the license appointment. Write it down as you go; the hours themselves will take care of themselves.

Frequently asked questions

How many supervised driving hours does Colorado require?

50 hours, including at least 10 at night, before a minor driver's license at 16. A parent or guardian signs a log certifying the hours.

How long do you have to hold a permit in Colorado?

12 months. That is the longest permit holding period in the country — most states use six months. It applies regardless of how quickly the 50 hours are completed.

How many night hours does Colorado require?

10 of the 50. Colorado does not publish a rigid clock definition, so treat night as actual darkness between sunset and sunrise. December darkness starts around 4:45 PM on the Front Range, which makes winter after-school drives count.

Does Colorado have an official driving log form?

Colorado requires a signed log of the 50 hours and publishes a log form alongside the affidavit of liability and guardianship. Download the current version from the DMV's teen drivers page rather than reusing an old copy.

Do I need driver education in Colorado?

It depends on when you start. At 15 you need a 30-hour driver education course to get a permit; at 15 and a half a 4-hour driver awareness program is enough; at 16 neither is required. None of these replace the 50 supervised hours.
